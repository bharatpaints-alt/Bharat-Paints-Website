Q: What is PU coating for wood?
A: PU (Polyurethane) is a hard, durable topcoat for wood.
Available in Matt, Satin and Gloss finish.
Waterproof, scratch resistant, long lasting.

Q: What is the difference between Matt, Satin and Gloss finish?
A: Matt — no shine, natural wood look, hides imperfections.
Satin — slight sheen, balanced look, most popular.
Gloss — high shine, bright look, easy to clean.

Q: Which finish is best for kitchen cabinets?
A: Gloss or Satin Duco finish — hard, washable, easy to clean.
Available in any colour from RAL shade card.

Q: Which coating for wooden doors?
A: PU Gloss or Satin topcoat over Epoxy base sealer.
For colour doors — Duco finish available in any colour.

Q: Which coating for gates and grills?
A: Epoxy 1K Grey Primer as base then PU topcoat.
For metallic finish — metallic PU available.

Q: What is Epoxy Clear Sealer — why is it important?
A: Epoxy Clear Sealer is applied before PU on wood.
It seals the wood grain and gives PU a strong base to bond with.
Skipping this step causes PU to peel. Very important first step.
